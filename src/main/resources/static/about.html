About RoadRescue

Roadside help that actually shows up.

RoadRescue connects stranded drivers with nearby professional mechanics — in real time, transparently, and without the guesswork. One request finds the nearest available shop, tracks the mechanic live, and keeps everyone informed.

What we do

📍

Find the nearest help

Your location is matched to the closest open shop, with automatic reassignment if a shop can't take the job.

🚗

Track every step

Live status updates and mechanic location sharing, so you're never left wondering when help will arrive.

🏪

Empower local shops

Mechanics receive, accept, and manage requests with date-filtered reports and PDF exports.

🛡️

Admin oversight

A full analytics dashboard keeps the platform healthy — requests, shops, and performance at a glance.

🤖

Nova, always online

An offline chatbot covering 50+ common vehicle problems, with one-tap helpline calling when you need it.

🔒

Secure by design

JWT-based sessions, BCrypt password hashing, and role-based access across customers, shops, and admins.
