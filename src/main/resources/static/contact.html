Quick answers

How do I request help?
Log in as a customer, enter your vehicle and issue, pick your location, and submit. The nearest open shop is assigned automatically.
Do I need internet for Nova?
No — Nova works completely offline and covers 50+ common roadside problems right on your device.
I own a shop. Can I join?
Yes. Register as a service partner from the home page with your branch locations and opening hours.
